Does your household make less than $89'000 a year?

One household appointment covers the taxpayer, spouse, and dependents.
Spouses filing Married Filing Separately are still covered by one appointment, even if two returns are prepared.


OR,
If your household makes more than $89'000 per year, are you or someone in your household:


65 years of age or older?
A veteran?
Active duty military?
An individual with disabilities?


If none of the above applies to you, you do not qualify for services with VITA.
