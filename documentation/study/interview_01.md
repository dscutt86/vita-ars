# Interview 1

### 1) Should the system support non-English speakers, and what languages are a priority?
a) Out-of-scope

### 2) Should the system allow the client to indicate a specific domain of tax assistance required to ensure an appropriate tax professional is scheduled? What are these domains or types of tax assistance that may be needed?
a) No

### 3) Should the system automatically screen clients, or should the system push a screening request to a volunteer or partner to complete?
a) Automatic

### 4) What are the exact criteria for screening?
a) Less than $89'000 in household income, or
65 years old or older, or
veteran with limited income, or
person with disabilities, or
person with limited English-speaking capabilities

### 5) What questions should be asked when ensuring that only one reservation is made per household?
a) This will be handled on-site. We can add a reminder in the reservation system to ensure clients understand that they may have only one system.

### 6) May clients have follow-ups? How should we handle cancellations?
a) Follow-ups must be coordinated with the site coordinator. Assume each appointment is completed within the 1-hour time block.

### 7) What information should be retained prior to and after an appointment? Should clients have accounts, for example?
a) No accounts. Appointment data kept for 3 years. Deleted after. No sensitive PII.
