# VITA ARS Study Part 1


## Problem Statement
Hartwick VITA & TCE site needs a consistent reservation process for its campus and mobile
tax-assistance sites. You will investigate the operation through provided documents and
individual interviews with the site operations manager, develop a supported set of
requirements, and use those findings in your individual final project.

Currently, clients arrange appointments through United Way 2-1-1, the local library, mobile-site partners, and the Hartwick phone line. These scheduling channels use a shared Google Calendar. However, broad editing access can make the schedule inconsistent. The program wants every channel to follow one reservation procedure, with preliminary screening before appointment selection.


## Background Info
The IRS’s Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA) and Tax Counseling for the Elderly (TCE)
programs offer free basic tax return preparation to qualified individuals. The VITA program has operated for over 50 years, offering free tax help to people who generally make $82,000 or less, persons with disabilities, and limited English-speaking taxpayers who need assistance in preparing their own tax returns. In addition to VITA, the TCE program offers free tax help, particularly for those who are 60 years of age and older, specializing in questions about pensions and retirement-related issues unique to seniors. While the IRS manages the VITA and TCE programs, the VITA/TCE sites are managed by IRS partners and staffed by their volunteers who want to make a difference in their communities. Hartwick College served as an IRS VITA tax preparation site since AY2019. In the past 7 tax seasons, the program has served over 8,000 families in upstate New York and contributed nearly $ 6 million in economic impact to local communities.


## Reservation Process
1) The client completes the site screening questions.
2) The client must pass screening before selecting an appointment date and time.
3) The client selects an available appointment. Its site, date, and time must be clear.
4) The client provides a full name, phone number, and ZIP code.
5) The reservation is recorded through the common booking process, subject to available capacity and the household appointment rule.


## Household Apppointment Rule
1) Each household may make only one appointment.
2) Clients may not reserve multiple appointments.
3) The restriction applies across the program, including bookings made through different channels or at different locations.
4) Confirm what the program means by a household, what its appointment covers, how an
existing household is recognized, and how cancellation or follow-up affects the rule. 
5) Do not independently reinterpret it as one appointment per site, one active appointment, or one appointment per tax year.

## Appointment Description
1) Each appointment lasts one hour and includes a brief taxpayer interview, tax preparation, and quality review.
2) Friday and Saturday service has a noon-1 p.m. break.
